Privacy

Private Call Captions processes only the microphone you choose, on this device. It does not capture system audio, record calls, send audio or transcripts to us, use analytics, or create an account.

Caption text, corrections, and exports stay on your device. You choose when to export and where the file is saved. The optional paid-license token is stored locally and is verified with Sociobot only when online.
