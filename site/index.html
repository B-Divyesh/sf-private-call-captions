A narrow tool for a sensitive call

What it does not do

  • It does not record calls or capture system audio.
  • It does not create an account or send captions to a transcription service.
  • It does not identify speakers or make meeting notes.

Captions can be wrong with noise, accents, names, or interruptions. Do not use them as emergency, legal, or medical transcription.
