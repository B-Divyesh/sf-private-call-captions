Privacy

Private Call Captions processes the microphone you choose on your device. It does not capture system audio, send audio or captions to us, record calls, use tracking, or create accounts.

Session text stays in memory until you export it or close the app. A purchased license token is stored locally. Sociobot checks it no more than daily while online.
