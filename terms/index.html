Terms

Private Call Captions is an accessibility aid, not a recording, legal transcription, or emergency service. Check captions against the speaker, especially with accents, names, noise, and critical information. Get consent before using captions where your call or workplace requires it.

The optional one-time unlock is sold by Sociobot/Dodo, merchant of record. Refunds are handled by that service. Accessibility features and CSV/TXT export are never paywalled.
